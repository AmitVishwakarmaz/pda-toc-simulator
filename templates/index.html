    HTML / XML Tag Nesting Validation

    Opening tags are pushed onto the stack; closing tags must match the top and pop it. If the stack is empty at the end, the document is well-formed.

    Example: <div> <p> <b> text </b> </p> </div>

    NLP — Shift-Reduce Syntax Parse

    Words are shifted (pushed) onto the stack. When the top symbols match a grammar rule, they are reduced (popped and replaced by the non-terminal). Accepts when the stack reduces to a single S.

    Supported: the, a, an, dog, cat, bird, fish, man, woman, child, boy, girl, tree, car, book, ball, chased, ate, saw, hit, liked, found, heard, loves, runs, catches, caught, big, small, red, old, young, quickly, slowly
